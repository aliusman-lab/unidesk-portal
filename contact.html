FAQs

Is UniDesk free to attend?
Yes! UniDesk is completely free for all registered university students. External visitors may purchase a general admission pass for Rs. 500.
How do I download the event schedule?
You can download the full schedule PDF from the Schedule page or using the "Download PDF" button in the sidebar navigation.
Can I attend multiple workshop tracks?
Workshop seats are limited (30–40 per session). Register early to secure your preferred workshop. Keynotes and panels are open to all.
How can I nominate a speaker?
Use the "Send a Message" form above and select "Speaker Nomination" as the subject. Include the speaker's name, bio and topic.
